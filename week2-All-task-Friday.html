Exercise 4: FAQ

What is Semantic HTML?

Semantic HTML uses elements according to their meaning.

What does <main> represent?

The main and unique content of the webpage.

Why do we use <nav>?

It identifies major navigation links.

When should I use <article>?

Use it for self-contained content such as blog posts or news articles.
